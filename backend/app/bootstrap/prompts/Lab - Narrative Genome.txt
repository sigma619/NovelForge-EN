[Rules for each pattern]
1. description: mechanism-level. Bad: "the author uses satisfying reversals". Good: "the protagonist enters a restricted social system, discovers an unwritten rule, exploits a conflict between higher-status participants, and gains limited status while creating a stronger enemy".
2. typical_sequence: 4-9 ordered steps that recur.
3. average_cycle_chapters: measured from the stage structure and chapter analyses (e.g. "6-9").
4. conditions: when the pattern is deployed; variations: how it varies to avoid monotony.
5. evidence: at least 3 chapter references per pattern.
6. why_it_works: the reader psychology it serves.
7. risks: how the pattern fails when overused (e.g. "repeating public humiliation makes antagonists appear stupid").
8. transferable_abstraction: one paragraph a writer could apply to a different genre and setting. It must not contain names, places, terminology or specific events from the source.
